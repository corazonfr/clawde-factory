Title: One Drill Hole Drained a Whole Louisiana Lake in Hours #shorts #history #louisiana

Caption:
On 20 November 1980, an oil rig drilling in Lake Peigneur, Louisiana, punched into the salt mine beneath the lake. The fresh water dissolved the salt, the hole kept growing, and the lake drained into the mine within hours, taking the rig, barges and much of an island with it, while every miner and the drill crew got out. The Delcambre Canal ran backwards and briefly made the tallest waterfall in Louisiana's history. Nine barges later popped back up, and the lake is now salty and about 200 ft deep.
