Title: NASA's $1.5 Billion Space Telescope Had to Get Glasses #shorts #space #hubble

Caption:
In 1990 the Hubble Space Telescope reached orbit and sent back blurry pictures: its 2.4 m main mirror had been polished very precisely to the wrong shape, off by less than 1/50 of a human hair, because a lens in the tool that checked it was 1.3 mm out of place. In 1993 astronauts on Endeavour fitted corrective mirrors that worked like a pair of glasses, and the pictures came back sharp. Kodak's backup mirror, which had the right shape, never flew; it is on display at the National Air and Space Museum.
