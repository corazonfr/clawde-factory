Title: The Town That Blew Up a Whale #shorts

Description:
In 1970, Oregon's highway department had a dead 45-foot sperm whale on the beach at Florence. Their plan: treat it like a boulder, blow it into tiny pieces with half a ton of dynamite, and let the seagulls clean up. A veteran with explosives training said twenty sticks would do it. They used twenty cases. Blubber rained down on the crowd a quarter of a mile away, one chunk crushed the roof of a brand-new car (bought at a sale promising "a whale of a deal"), and the gulls were scared off. Today Oregon buries beached whales where they land, and in 2020 Florence named a new park Exploding Whale Memorial Park.
