Title: One of the World's Mightiest Warships Sank Before Leaving the Harbour #shorts #history #sweden

Caption:
On 10 August 1628 the Swedish warship Vasa set off on her maiden voyage in Stockholm harbour. Top-heavy with two full decks of heavy guns, she heeled over in a gust, took water through her open gunports and sank after sailing just over a kilometre, less than half an hour into the voyage. A stability test before sailing, thirty men running across the deck, had been stopped because she rocked so much. The Baltic's low salinity kept shipworms away, so she was raised almost intact in 1961 and is now the most visited museum in Scandinavia.
