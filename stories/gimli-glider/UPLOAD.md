Title: A Jet Ran Out of Fuel Mid-Flight Because of Pounds vs Kilos #shorts #aviation #history

Caption:
In 1983, Air Canada Flight 143, a brand-new metric Boeing 767, ran out of fuel at 41,000 feet. With its fuel gauge broken, the fuel was worked out by hand using pounds instead of kilograms, so it carried less than half of what it needed. The captain, a glider pilot, glided it onto a former air base at Gimli, Manitoba, where a race was underway; nobody was seriously hurt, and the jet flew for another 25 years as the Gimli Glider.
