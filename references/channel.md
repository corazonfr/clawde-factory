## Why variety matters more than polish

TikTok's system reads the same template with swapped text as repetitive content
and quietly limits distribution. A channel posting one scene daily with a new
caption will look fine for two weeks and then flatline for reasons that never show
up in the analytics.

Scene count needed = (posts per day) x 3, minimum. At 2 posts/day that's 6
templates. At 5 posts/day it's 15. Do not raise the cadence before the scene
count is there — that is the exact move that flatlines a channel.

## Hook formulas

Rotate these. Don't run the same shape twice in a row.

1. **Reversal** — sets up an expectation, breaks it in the last second
2. **Escalation** — small problem compounds until it's absurd
3. **Deadpan reaction** — Clawde reacts to something offscreen, reveal at the end
4. **Loop bait** — last frame flows into the first so replays feel seamless
5. **POV** — "pov: you..." framing, character does the thing to camera

## Caption rules

- First 3 words carry it — the rest gets truncated in feed
- 2–4 hashtags, not 15
- No engagement bait ("comment below!") — reads as spam and gets suppressed
- Caption should add a beat, not narrate what's visibly happening
